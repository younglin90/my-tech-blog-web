I. Introduction
Background and Motivation
Metal particle are high-density energetic material
Several advantages : high enthalpy per volume, acoustic damping, easy transferability and clean & reusable combustion products
Utilized in aerospace industry to increase a specific impulse of propulsion system
A discrete burning of a small particle is an ideal situation of dust combustion for its fast reaction
However, particle agglomeration occurred at surface of solid fuel
Extend the total reaction time and decreases burning velocity of dust flame
Sippel, T. R., Son, S. F., & Groven, L. J. (2014). Aluminum agglomeration reduction in a composite propellant using tailored Al/PTFE particles.
