5.1.2 The effect of flame fronts on turbulence
 Flame modifies the turbulent flow field.
-After ignition, kinetic viscosity and local Reynolds numbers change
(a turbulent flow might become laminar after ignition)
-Flow acceleration through the flame front is important effect.
(The vorticity field is also affected by velocity and density
changes leading to the so-called "flame-generated turbulence.")
 Instantaneous balance equation of vorticity vector
vortex stretching (I), viscous dissipation (II), density
changes (III) and the baroclinic torque (IV)
 For low vortex strength, a counter-rotating flame-generated
vorticity was observed
 Vortex strength is large, the vortex crosses the flame front and
remains almost unaffected.
